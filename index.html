Where Zoupr Systems Is Headed

Current State → Long-Term Goal
Paper first. Real markets later.
Zoupr Systems is currently being developed and tested in paper mode. The purpose of paper testing is to validate the system, improve reliability, and measure performance before risking real capital.
Stage 01 • Current

Paper Trading

Run the bot against real market data while using simulated balances and simulated execution so changes can be tested without risking funds.

Stage 02 • Intended

Real Solana Memecoin Trading

The intended next major step is controlled live execution on Solana memecoins once the paper system is stable enough to justify risking real capital.

Stage 03 • Long Term

Automated Nasdaq Strategies

A longer-term goal is to expand Zoupr Systems into automated Nasdaq-market strategies, with separate logic and testing built specifically for traditional markets.

These are development goals, not promises of profitability or launch dates. Live trading would only be considered after testing, risk controls, execution reliability, and performance are strong enough to justify the move.
